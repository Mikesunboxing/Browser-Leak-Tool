Router Setup Instructions

  1. Access Gateway: Open your web browser and log in to your router gateway IP (typically 192.168.1.1 or 192.168.0.1).
  2. Find Settings: Locate the WAN, Internet, or DHCP Settings menu.
  3. Set Static DNS: Switch your DNS setting mode from ISP Default / Dynamic to Static or Manual.
  4. Enter Addresses: Paste the Primary and Secondary IPv4/IPv6 addresses of your chosen provider tier.
  5. Enable Encrypted DNS (Optional): If your router supports DoT (DNS-over-TLS) or DoH (DNS-over-HTTPS), paste the provider's Hostname into the DoT field.
  6. Save & Reboot: Save settings and restart your router or renew the DHCP lease on your computer.

Verification & Cache Flush

Clear your Windows local DNS resolver cache in PowerShell:

ipconfig /flushdns
